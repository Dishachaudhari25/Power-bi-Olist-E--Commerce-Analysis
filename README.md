# 📊 Olist E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales, revenue, customer reviews, payment methods, product categories, and geographical performance of the **Olist E-Commerce dataset**.

The dashboard provides interactive visualizations that help understand business performance and identify important sales and customer trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
* Track total orders and total revenue.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Analyze monthly order trends.
* Understand sales distribution across Brazilian states and cities.
* Analyze different payment methods and their contribution to revenue.
* Evaluate customer review ratings by product category.
* Identify high-performing seller cities and states.
* Provide interactive filtering using Year, Product Category, and Order Status.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Measures and calculations
* **Microsoft Bing Maps / Map Visual**
* **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset**

# 📑 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Sales Overview

The Sales Overview page provides a complete summary of the e-commerce sales performance.

### Key KPIs

* **Total Orders:** 99.441K
* **Average Order Value:** R$137.75
* **Average Customer Rating:** 4.09
* **Total Revenue:** R$13.59M

### Visualizations

* Top 10 Product Categories by Revenue
* Monthly Order Trend
* Order Distribution by Brazilian State
* Payment Value by Payment Type and Year
* Product Category Filter
* Year Filter
* Order Status Filter

### Key Insights

* **Health & Beauty** is the highest-revenue product category at approximately **R$1.26M**.
* **Watches & Gifts** generated approximately **R$1.21M**.
* **Bed, Bath & Table** generated approximately **R$1.04M**.
* Monthly order volume shows noticeable fluctuations throughout the year.
* **August** records one of the highest monthly order counts at approximately **10.8K orders**.
* **December** has the lowest order count among the displayed months at approximately **5.7K orders**.

## 2️⃣ 🌎 Geographic Analysis

The Geographic Analysis page focuses on the geographical distribution of sellers and revenue across Brazil.

### Visualizations

* Order Distribution by Brazilian State
* Top 10 Seller Cities by Revenue
* Revenue/Sales by Seller State
* Year Filter

### Key Insights

* **São Paulo (SP)** is the strongest-performing seller state, with approximately **R$8.8M** in sales.
* **Paraná (PR)** follows with approximately **R$1.3M**.
* **Minas Gerais (MG)** contributes approximately **R$1.0M**.
* **Rio de Janeiro (RJ)** contributes approximately **R$0.8M**.
* **São Paulo city** is the top seller city, generating approximately **R$2.7M** in revenue.
* Other high-performing cities include **Ibtinga, Curitiba, Rio de Janeiro, Guarulhos, and Ribeirão Preto**.

## 3️⃣ 💳 Payments & Reviews

The Payments & Reviews page analyzes payment behavior and customer satisfaction.

### Visualizations

* Payment Value by Type and Year
* Payment Type Mix
* Average Review Score by Product Category
* Year Filter

### Payment Insights

Total payment value analyzed is approximately **R$160.09M**.

| Payment Type | Total Value |
| ------------ | ----------: |
| Credit Card  |   R$125.42M |
| Boleto       |    R$28.69M |
| Voucher      |     R$3.79M |
| Debit Card   |     R$2.18M |
| Not Defined  |         R$0 |

### Key Insights

* **Credit Card** is the dominant payment method, contributing approximately **78.34%** of the payment value.
* **Boleto** is the second-largest payment method at approximately **17.92%**.
* Voucher payments contribute approximately **2.37%**.
* Debit card contributes a smaller share of the total payment value.
* The overall payment value increased across the displayed years.

### Customer Review Insights

The highest average review scores are observed for categories such as:

* **CDs, DVDs & Musicals** – 4.67
* **Fashion, Bags & Accessories** – 4.50
* **Leisure & Interests** – 4.46
* **Construction & Tools** – 4.43
* **Technical Books** – 4.41

The high review scores indicate generally positive customer satisfaction across several product categories.

---

# 📈 Overall Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:

1. **São Paulo is the major business hub**, leading both state-level and city-level performance.
2. **Health & Beauty** is the highest-revenue product category among the displayed categories.
3. **Credit cards dominate customer payments**, accounting for more than three-fourths of the total payment value.
4. Customer satisfaction is relatively high, with an overall **average rating of 4.09**.
5. Order volumes fluctuate significantly by month, indicating seasonal variations in customer demand.
6. The dashboard highlights strong revenue concentration in a small number of states and cities.
7. Product categories with high customer ratings can provide opportunities for further sales and marketing strategies.
